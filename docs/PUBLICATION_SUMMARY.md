# A2 MissionLab Publication Summary

A2 MissionLab is an evidence-grounded public reconstruction of Artemis II mission operations and engineering.

## Validated scale

```text
25 indexed reference datasets
81 normalized public sources
24 shared mission events
10 decision threads
6 postflight engineering records
146 searchable analyst records
8 deterministic analyst tools
8 deterministic answer routes
```

## Engineering philosophy

```text
evidence over speculation

physics over fake telemetry

known unknowns over invented answers

deterministic calculation over opaque inference

provenance over presentation
```
